# Team

Eclipse CSI is developed in the open by the project leads and committers of the Eclipse project
[technology.csi](https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/technology.csi), elected following the Eclipse Development
Process, with the contributions of everyone who wants to help.

## The Eclipse Foundation Security Team { #security-team }

The Eclipse Foundation Security Team is part of the staff of the Eclipse Foundation. It strengthens the security of
the Eclipse projects and of the services of the Foundation: it triages the vulnerability reports and brings in the
projects, operates the [CVE Numbering Authority](cves.md) of the Eclipse Foundation, runs the
[AI-assisted security reviews](ai-security-reviews.md), gives [trainings](learn.md#trainings), and provides the
projects with tools and guidelines, many of which are developed here.

<p class="csi-links" markdown>[:lucide-arrow-right: Its members, on eclipse.org](https://www.eclipse.org/security/team/)</p>

!!! warning "Check who contacts you"

    The Security Team contacts committers only through the accounts listed on
    [its page](https://www.eclipse.org/security/team/), which also gives the permissions that its members may hold on
    your repositories. None of its members will ever ask for your private credentials: if someone claiming to be from
    the team does, it is not them.
